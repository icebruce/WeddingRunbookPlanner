Wedding Runbook Planner · Mobile mockup

Reference for the phone layout (390 pt wide). Built from the real seed timeline; card positions come from real start and end times at the approved scale. Screens scroll. On a phone, swipe sideways between them.

Approved rules shown here
  • Scale: 20 px per 5 minutes. Lines every 5 minutes, labels at 15, 30 and 60. Card edges sit exactly on their times.
  • Card rows: title, time, location, stage, people. Short cards drop people, then stage, then location, then time. Faint dots bottom-right mean something is hidden.
  • Phone cards stay quiet. Selecting one shows the toolbar and its handles: top and bottom for flexible activities, bottom only for fixed ones.
  • Colour roles: phase colours for stages, blue for selection, red only for conflict and delete, black for fixed, green for live.
  • Day-of view: automatic on the wedding date and when status is Final, view only until Edit.
  • Light is the default. Dark is an in-app switch (menu or Plan settings).
